OPERATIVE SIGNAL_RSA_03 — Toy RSA Factorization & Decryption +250 XP
TARGET RSA PARAMETERS:
n = 3233 (public modulus) e = 17 (public exponent) c = 2557 (ciphertext — an encrypted integer)

Factor n into p × q. Compute Euler’s totient φ(n)=(p−1)(q−1). Find d = e−¹ mod φ(n) using the Extended Euclidean Algorithm. Decrypt: m = c^d mod n. The plaintext m is a small positive integer — that integer IS the flag’s numeric component.

n = 3233 = 61 × 53. φ = 60 × 52 = 3120. Extended Euclidean: 17 × 2753 ≡ 1 (mod 3120), so d = 2753. m = 2557^2753 mod 3233. The answer to life, the universe, and everything.

ELITE SIGNAL_ENT_05 — Shannon Entropy Oracle +400 XP
SIGNAL CAPTURE STATISTICS:
Distribution: 10 distinct symbols Each symbol probability: p = 0.10 (10%) Formula: H(X) = −∑ p(x) log₂ p(x)

Calculate the Shannon entropy of this uniform 10-symbol source in bits. Round to 2 decimal places. High entropy ≈ encrypted or compressed data. Low entropy ≈ structured/human-readable data. Your calculated value IS the flag’s numeric component.

H = −10 × (0.1 × log₂(0.1)) = −10 × (0.1 × −3.32193…) = 10 × 0.332193 ≈ 3.32 bits. This equals log₂(10) — maximum entropy for 10 equally-likely symbols.
FLAG FORMAT: FLAG{entropy_?point??} — e.g. FLAG{entropy_3point32}
