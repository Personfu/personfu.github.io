▶ HOW THE TRIAL WORKS

1. Read the dossier

Every mission ships with a scenario, a simulated lab, source artifacts and three escalating hints. No cheat-sheets, just real tradecraft.

2. Solve in your head

Each lab is reproducible offline. Use your terminal, browser DevTools, CyberChef — whatever you bring. The lab snippets give you everything you need.

3. Submit the flag

Your answer is hashed in-browser and checked against an embedded SHA-256. We never see your guesses; flags never appear in source.
