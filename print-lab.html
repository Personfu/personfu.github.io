Why fabrication matters

Cybersecurity hardware work is physical, not just code. Cable strain relief, badge shells, sensor mounts, RF antenna clips, and labeled test fixtures turn a chaotic lab into an operational system. This page gives the player a build/progress loop that supports the Mars launch story.
