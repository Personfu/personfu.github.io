Safety Boundary

All tool walkthroughs are fictional, sandboxed, and defensive. They teach how systems work, how mistakes happen, and how to patch them. They do not target real networks, real devices, or real credentials.

Think of this as a simulator: the Metasploit-style console is a dry-run mission planner, the Flipper/Proxmark/Hak5-inspired tools are collectible lab benches, and the launch is a story wrapper for skill progression.
