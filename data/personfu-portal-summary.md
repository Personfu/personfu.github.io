## Boundary

Allowed: owned infrastructure; written authorization; contracted assessment; bug bounty scope; CTF or synthetic target; defensive validation and reporting

Blocked: credential theft; persistence; stealth or evasion; live-target exploitation without authorization; doxxing or personal-data abuse; destructive payloads
