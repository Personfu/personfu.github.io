F12 RSA-toy decryption PENDING

Ciphertext c = 352, public key (e=17, n=3233), private exponent d = 2753. Recover the plaintext integer m = cd mod n.

m = pow(c, d, n)
  = 352^2753 mod 3233
How do I compute this without huge numbers?

Use repeated squaring with a modulus after every multiplication. In Python, pow(352, 2753, 3233) performs the same calculation.
