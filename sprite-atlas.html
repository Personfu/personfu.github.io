SPRITE ATLAS ARMORY

This page turns the three uploaded mega-sheets into a real asset pipeline. It looks for repo-hosted images under /assets/sprites/gemini/, and also lets you import the attached PNGs locally for immediate preview. Once the files are committed at the expected paths, every visitor gets them automatically.

Asset Mapping

  • Items sheet: consumables, rare loot, drones, non-human entities.
  • Operative sheet: avatar classes, gear, hardware icons, adversaries.
  • Vehicle sheet: transit, hovercraft, orbital shuttles, kiosks, movement systems.
Binary upload note: this environment can read your attached PNGs, but the GitHub connector available here writes UTF-8 source files, not binary PNG blobs. The game now has the loader and expected paths; the final art drop is just committing the three PNGs into the listed folder.
