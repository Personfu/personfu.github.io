How These Sheets Become Gameplay

Persona Layer
Operative sheet feeds avatar identity, faction look, vanity slots, and threat silhouettes.
Physical Layer
Items and hardware sheets feed lab bench props, fabrication rewards, and safe training gear.
Logical Layer
Atlas selections persist locally and connect to the existing CyberWorld save/profile engine.
Mission Layer
Vehicles/transit become unlockable movement systems for Mars launch districts and orbital support.
