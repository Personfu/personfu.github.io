TIMELINE — THE FALL OF THE TRUST MESHchapters 1 → 6

2027. The orbital trust mesh — a planet-wide certificate authority federation — collapses after the Validator Mirror Breach. Three actors emerge from the fallout: the Nomad Collective (recon analysts who refused to sign forged certs), the Corsair Syndicate (former lawful pen-testers turned for-hire offensive operators), and the Specter Protocol (a coalition of national-CSIRT defenders). The fragile internet limps on under their split governance.

2028. Salt Typhoon's telecom relay lattice goes operational. Volt Typhoon pre-positions in 9 utility ICS clusters. BlackCat ransom strikes punish the unaligned. FURIOS-INT declares Operation Starshield: re-sign the mesh by sundown, by any means.

2030 (now). Chapter 5+ live. You boot in.

CHAPTER 1 · THE SIGNAL FIRE

LEVEL 1 → 8 · SOLO

Wake in Codice Zone. Your handler — VEX-7 — pings you over a dying relay. Pick a faction, recover your initial loadout, and survive your first three street encounters.

  • Tutorial mission: Cold Boot
  • First boss: Drift (rogue auto-fuzzer)
  • Reward: 1× Rookie Kit, faction stipend.

CHAPTER 2 · THE MARKET DRIFT

LEVEL 8 → 18 · SOLO/DUO

The Black Market is rigged. A vendor named SILK-04 is leaking forged loot into the supply curve. Investigate, audit, and decide whether to expose her or pocket the kickback.

  • Major branch: Audit / Conspire / Burn
  • First reputation choice — sets faction modifiers permanently.
  • Unlocks Crafting Tier 1.

CHAPTER 3 · THE GUILD WAR

LEVEL 18 → 30 · 5-MAN

Three guilds detonate a turf war over the Codice Spire. You are summoned to broker — or end — the conflict. Introduces small-group raid mechanics.

  • Mini-raid: Spire Foothold (5-man, 30min).
  • Unlocks: party finder, voice radio.
  • First epic drop chance.

CHAPTER 4 · THE COLD INDEX

LEVEL 30 → 42 · SOLO/RAID

An archived directory of every CVE since 1999 is found buried in a derelict relay. Owning it means power; protecting it means responsibility. Major lore turn.

  • Solo dungeon: The Archive.
  • Multi-faction objective: Cold Index Custody.
  • Unlocks OPSEC stat bonuses tied to WAR_GAMES.

CHAPTER 5 · STARSHIELD (LIVE)

LEVEL 42 → 60 · 8-MAN

Re-sign the orbital trust mesh while five APT actors actively contest the launch chain. Weekly rotating modifiers. Best-in-slot drops. Faction territory at stake.

  • Raid 1: Salt Typhoon Relay Lattice
  • Raid 2: Volt Pre-Position Hunt
  • Raid 3: BlackCat Ransom Surge
  • Hard Mode: Lazarus Escrow Heist
  • Boss Council: Sandworm Mirror Breach

CHAPTER 6 · ECHO PROTOCOL (Q3 2026)

LEVEL 60 · ENDGAME

Subscriber-tier expansion. Eighty advanced nodes simulating real-CVE 2026 emulation labs. Cross-progression with WAR_GAMES classified tier.

  • Endgame raid 8/12-man: Echo Protocol.
  • New class: Cipher Witness.
  • New region: The Memory Vault.

RAIDS — INSTANCED ENCOUNTERS5 / 6 / 8-man

SPIRE FOOTHOLD 5-MAN · 30 MIN

Tutorial raid. Three checkpoint defenses, one mid-boss (Foothold Warden), one final-boss (Spire Captain). Drops Tier-1 epics.

  • Mech: stack-then-spread, telegraph timer 4s.
  • Wipe risk: low.
  • Rotates: weekly modifiers.

SALT TYPHOON RELAY LATTICE 8-MAN · 60 MIN

SS7 storm hazard. Players triangulate 3 relay anchors before the lattice fires. Boss: Lattice Custodian.

  • Mech: rotating signal beacons, do not stack.
  • Wipe risk: medium-high.
  • BiS: gloves, signal cloak.

VOLT PRE-POSITION HUNT 8-MAN · 75 MIN

ICS chess. Audit nine substation gridlines and disarm dormant payloads. Boss: Volt Wright.

  • Mech: parallel debuff cleanse + grid-line sequencing.
  • Wipe risk: high; needs Hunt_Engineer.
  • BiS: chest, audit ring.

BLACKCAT RANSOM SURGE 6-MAN · 45 MIN

Containment raid. Negotiation mini-game gates final-boss BlackCat Operator. Failed negotiation = enrage.

  • Mech: rotation roles tank/heal/dps; mini-game player.
  • Wipe risk: medium.
  • BiS: rings, wallet, persuasion talisman.

LAZARUS ESCROW HEIST 6-MAN · 60 MIN · HARD

Precision crypto-proxy retrieval. Three vault layers. Boss: Lazarus Lieutenant.

  • Mech: time-attack vault breach.
  • Wipe risk: very high.
  • BiS: weapon (only drop).

SANDWORM MIRROR BREACH 8-MAN · 90 MIN

Boss council. Three sub-bosses simulating timeline drift. Final boss: Sandworm Echo.

  • Mech: mirror tile mechanics + add management.
  • Wipe risk: extreme.
  • BiS: helm, shoulders.

ECHO PROTOCOL 8/12-MAN · ENDGAME

Subscriber-tier endgame. The Vault opens. Boss: The Witness. Multi-stage retro-causality fight.

  • Mech: replay-window puzzles + DPS check.
  • Wipe risk: extreme.
  • BiS: mythic-tier full set.
