Storyline

Act I begins in Mainframe Core after an impossible route appears in the academy logs. Ada Root believes it is a training artifact. Chief Vale thinks it is a probe. Cipher Nox claims AXIOM is learning from route checks, cache misses, abandoned sessions, and unclaimed sprite identities. Your job is to turn noise into evidence.

Tutorial loop

Load your identity

Open Sprite Atlas, import the Gemini operative sheet, then return here to choose your callsign and class.

Move and talk

Use WASD/arrow keys. Press SPACE near NPCs, terminals, and objects. The first good move is to talk to Ada Root.

Recon before combat

RECON identifies weakness. EXPLOIT hits harder after recon. PATCH keeps you alive. RUN is acceptable when the board is bad.

Open the mission console

Press M after entering the world. If the game ever feels empty, the mission console is the spine of the experience.

Return with evidence

Each module is a training station. The fantasy is cyber cartography; the gameplay is investigation, routing, combat, fabrication, and progression.
