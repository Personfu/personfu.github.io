How to read these QRs

Use any modern phone camera or zbarimg:

zbarimg cve_index.png
zbarimg jwt_decoder_demo.png
zbarimg mitigation_playbook.png

The cve_index.png QR carries a compact JSON document; the per-CVE QRs deep-link to NVD; the JWT QR is an HS256 token signed with the key cyberworld — decode it, verify it, find the flag.
