AGENCY MAP

A practical map of Arizona government agencies and where public documentation usually lives: statutes, budgets, meeting packets, procurement files, GIS, court records, corporations, and infrastructure programs.

← Vault

State layer

  • Governor and agency directory: executive structure and appointments.
  • Arizona Legislature: bills, statutes, budget, committee files, hearing calendars.
  • Secretary of State: elections, rules, public notices, business services, archives.
  • Corporation Commission: entity records, utilities, securities, dockets.
  • Department of Administration: procurement, risk, facilities, accounting, HR.
  • Arizona Courts: rules, opinions, case information, forms, court structure.

County layer

  • Recorders: recorded documents, election records, maps, official filings.
  • Assessors: parcels, valuations, land-use clues, GIS links.
  • Supervisors: agendas, minutes, budgets, capital projects, ordinances.
  • Sheriff and courts: public information portals, jail policies, court calendars.
  • Procurement: solicitations, vendor lists, award records, contract documents.
