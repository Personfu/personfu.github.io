Arizona has 30 legislative districts. Each district elects one senator and two representatives. The live official roster changes, so GovINT should link the live roster, preserve snapshots by date, and build district files from official sources rather than stale hand-copied lists.

Reliability labels

LabelUse
VerifiedPrimary official record directly supports the statement.
OfficialAgency, court, legislature, or institution published the statement.
CredibleReputable secondary source, still checked against records.
DisputedCompeting explanations or conflicting source accounts.
FolkloreCultural story or community legend without firm documentation.
UnresolvedInsufficient evidence to close the file.
GovINT treats conspiracy and UAP material as public-history and source-analysis content, not proof of claims without primary records.
