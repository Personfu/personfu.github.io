FOIA is an evidence workflow.

Good requests are narrow, tied to a real office or record system, and written so an agency records officer can search without guessing. GovINT should use FOIA and state public-records law to document budgets, contracts, policies, meeting records, maps, reports, and public programs.

The site should not claim access to non-public secrets. The value is organizing public records better than normal readers have time to do.

Request formula

  1. Define the record: contract, map, memo, meeting minutes, invoice, policy, dataset, report, or procurement file.
  2. Identify the custodian: agency, department, county office, city clerk, board, commission, or records division.
  3. Limit the range: date window, project name, contract number, facility name, grant number, docket, or public meeting.
  4. Ask for electronic copies where available.
  5. Request a fee estimate before processing large searches.
  6. Track response date, production format, and appeal path.

Arizona public-records targets

  • City clerks for council packets, minutes, ordinances, zoning, and contracts.
  • County recorders for recorded instruments and election-related public files.
  • County assessors for parcels, valuation, and map data.
  • Procurement offices for solicitations, bids, awards, and vendor contracts.
  • State boards and commissions for dockets, meeting notices, enforcement records, and rulemaking.
  • Transportation and water agencies for maps, capital projects, public notices, and environmental material.

Reusable request templates

Use caseTemplate
Contract filePlease provide the solicitation, winning proposal, award notice, executed contract, amendments, invoices, and payment records for [project/contract] from [date] to [date].
Policy historyPlease provide documents showing the creation, approval, revision history, and current version of policies related to [program/topic] from [date] to [date].
Meeting packetPlease provide the agenda, packet, minutes, exhibits, staff reports, presentations, and public comments for meetings where [topic/project] was discussed.
GIS layerPlease provide electronic GIS layers, map exports, metadata, and public documentation for [facility/program/project/geography].
Grant trailPlease provide the grant application, award letter, budget narrative, performance reports, reimbursement requests, and closeout documents for [grant/program].

GovINT report structure

  • Claim or research question.
  • Known public facts.
  • Source chain with links and dates.
  • Records requested and agency custodian.
  • Response status and file inventory.
  • Reliability label and unresolved gaps.
  • Plain-English summary for public readers.
