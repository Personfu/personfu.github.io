Folklore is not useless. It just needs labels.

Arizona's anomaly stories are part public record, part media archive, part oral history, part tourism, and part unresolved claim. GovINT should not flatten everything into belief or debunking. It should separate event timeline, witness claims, official statements, media coverage, records requested, and open questions.

Reliability labels

LabelMeaning
VerifiedPrimary record confirms the factual point.
OfficialGovernment or institution published the statement.
CredibleReputable source, still not independently confirmed.
PossiblePlausible but incomplete source chain.
FolkloreCultural/community claim without firm documentation.
UnresolvedInsufficient evidence to close the question.

How the dossier should read

  • Open with a short event timeline.
  • Separate what was observed from what was inferred.
  • Link public statements, reports, media archives, and official records.
  • List requested records and missing files.
  • Keep a neutral reliability label next to each claim.
  • Offer multiple explanations when the evidence supports uncertainty.
