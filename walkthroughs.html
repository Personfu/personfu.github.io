Flipper/Proxmark-Style Lab: Badge Literacy

Compare fictional badge formats. Learn the difference between identification, authentication, encryption, and authorization. The player earns RF skill by explaining why copying identifiers is not access control.

Hak5/Pineapple-Style Lab: Rogue AP Defense

Spot suspicious SSIDs, certificate mismatch warnings, captive portal traps, and weak guest segmentation in a toy launch-center network. The win condition is a hardened BYOD design.

Pager + Telemetry Lab

Classify fictional pager alerts from the Mars payload team. Separate routine status, warning, critical, and false positive events. Then write an escalation rule.

3-D Printing Lab

Prepare a rover antenna bracket: choose infill, check orientation, verify tolerances, and document why payload hardware needs repeatable fabrication notes.
