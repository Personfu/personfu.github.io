⚡ FLLC CYBER ARCADE // OPERATIVE INTEL

Five fully playable arcade games — all free, no account needed, works offline. High scores and XP feed into the CYBERWORLD MMORPG so your arcade performance matters on the grid.

Games in the Arcade

  • ▶ CYBER_SNAKE — Classic grid-based snake reimagined on a neon data grid. Eat packets, grow your trace, don't loop back on yourself.
  • ☠ PACKET_DEFENDER — Side-scrolling turret shooter. Intercept enemy payloads before they breach the firewall. Enemies get faster with every wave.
  • ■ HEX_BREAKER — Breakout-style brick smasher across encrypted hex blocks. Clear every layer to unlock the next tier.
  • ↯ WIRE_RUNNER — Endless runner through the underground network. Jump firewalls, dodge NAT blocks, survive as long as possible.
  • ✦ CYBERWORLD_RPG — Full Phaser 3 RPG with faction selection, stealth ops, minigames, and a 9-zone world map. No login required — guest operatives welcome.

XP + Credits

  • Every high score in the arcade awards XP to your CYBERWORLD operative profile automatically.
  • XP is calculated from your score — the higher you rank, the more XP carries over to the MMORPG.
  • Scores persist in your browser. No cloud account needed. Play anywhere, including offline.
  • Scores sync to the CYBERWORLD leaderboard when you are online and have an operative handle.

▶ CYBER_SNAKE // SIGNAL TRACE PROTOCOL

Navigate the data grid. Collect packets to grow your trace. Every collision ends the run — speed scales with length.

  • Arrow keys or WASD to steer. R to restart. P to pause.
  • Score scales with length at collection time. Longer snake = higher multiplier.
  • Best score is saved locally and awards XP to your CYBERWORLD operative.

⚡ OPERATIVE ARCADE STATS

All scores are stored locally. High score XP bridges to your CYBERWORLD operative when you visit the MMORPG. The arcade is always free, always offline-capable.

  • CYBER_SNAKE: Score / 50 = XP awarded per new high score.
  • PACKET_DEFENDER: Score / 50 = XP. Survive longer for exponentially higher XP.
  • HEX_BREAKER: Score / 50 = XP. Full board clears grant a bonus XP pulse.
  • WIRE_RUNNER: Distance / 5 = XP. Endurance multiplier kicks in after 500m.
  • CYBERWORLD_RPG: XP awarded inside the RPG directly. Missions and minigames count.
