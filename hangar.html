        SORTIE BRIEFING

        CyberWorld Black Hangar is a Stormcore terminal exhibit. Every airframe shown here is fictional in-world lore for the operative academy. No real classified information is reproduced. Specs are speculative fictions inspired by USAF NGAD reporting (F-47), Navy F/A-XX, and decades of UAP/ARV community documentation including TR-3B and SR-91 Aurora mythos.

        HOW TO USE
        1. Select an exhibit from the fleet list in any tab to inspect it.
        2. HANGAR — bay scene with live wireframe, spec sheet, and status readout.
        3. WIREFRAME — ISO/ortho/cutaway mesh inspection. Tune tessellation, toggle callouts and blueprint grid.
        4. ENGINEERING — full propulsion, sensors, structure, and armament dossiers with performance envelope chart.
        5. COCKPIT — set throttle, toggle gear and canopy. Hit LAUNCH to return to CyberWorld grid.
